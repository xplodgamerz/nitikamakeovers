ABOUT NITIKA MAKEOVERS

Nitika Makeovers is a unisex salon and academy in Ranbir Singh Pura, Jammu, bringing beauty and grooming together under one roof for women and men alike.

From hair services and nail services to facials and beauty packages, every visit is built around personalised attention, a hygienic setting and a comfortable, welcoming atmosphere.

With a professional approach, we care for everyday grooming needs as well as special occasions, so you can leave feeling polished and confident.
